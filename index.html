Leadership

Ps Sphiwe Magcanya

Founder

His Word Christian Centre exists to build a people who know God's Word, learn His will and live according to His ways.

Pastor Sphiwe Magcanya is the founding pastor of His Word Christian Centre, a vibrant ministry committed to lifting Jesus up through the reading, teaching, and living of His Word. He is a passionate preacher, teacher, pastor, chaplain, qualified Marriage Officer, and Commissioner of Oaths, with a strong heart for developing people, strengthening families, and building healthy, Christ-centred communities.

Known for his practical, thought-provoking approach to Scripture, Pastor Magcanya combines sound biblical teaching with a contemporary perspective on faith, leadership, relationships, and everyday life. His ministry carries a particular emphasis on seeing people restored, healed, and matured in Christ. His life and ministry are anchored in this simple conviction: His Will. His Way. His Word.

Beyond the pulpit, Pastor Magcanya is a devoted husband to his beloved wife and a loving father. His family is a treasured part of his life, grounding him in the values of love, faith, and commitment that he so passionately teaches. Together, they share in the journey of faith, serving as a reminder that ministry begins not only in the church but also in the home.
